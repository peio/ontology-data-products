# Literature

Background material for the workshop. The papers are the methodology sources
behind how the AI Ontologist skills gather requirements and build schemas; the
slide decks are Graphwise material that applies that methodology and makes the
business case for semantic layers and knowledge graphs.

## Slide decks

| File | Content | Slides |
|------|---------|--------|
| [Ontology Development 101 — Graphwise.pptx](<Ontology Development 101 — Graphwise.pptx>) | The Noy & McGuinness method as nine phases (scope and competency questions, reuse, term enumeration, naming conventions, classes, properties, logical axioms, instances, evaluation), with each phase's inputs and outputs and the feedback paths between them. | 18 |
| [GW-Summary of Claude Self Service for Analytics.pptx](<GW-Summary of Claude Self Service for Analytics.pptx>) | Summary of Anthropic's post on self-service data analytics with Claude: the bottleneck is mapping a question to the right governed entity, not query generation; the three causes of wrong answers (ambiguity, staleness, retrieval failure) and the layered stack that addresses them. Closes with where a knowledge graph platform fits. | 12 |
| [NLQ Sem Layer - Measuring Data Pipeline Improvements.pptx](<NLQ Sem Layer - Measuring Data Pipeline Improvements.pptx>) | Modelled business case for a semantic layer with natural-language query, delivered with metadata and governance standardisation: 22 value drivers across data discovery, analytics and interpretation. | 10 |
| [Token-Savings-deck.pptx](Token-Savings-deck.pptx) | Token consumption experiment: 11 questions over a GraphDB knowledge graph built from SEC EDGAR, USPTO and SIC/NAICS data, comparing context stuffing (4.0M tokens) with knowledge-graph retrieval (75K tokens), a 54.2x reduction. | 15 |
